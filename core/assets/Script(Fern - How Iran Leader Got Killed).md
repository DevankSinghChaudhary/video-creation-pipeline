# Israeli and US Military Strikes on Iran: A Detailed Overview

## ✈️ Military Operations and Attacks

Early morning Israeli fighter jets launched missiles targeting a key compound in Tehran, killing Iran’s Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Ham and several top officials.

The strikes were meticulously planned with CIA and Israeli intelligence tracking Iranian leadership movements for months.

Multiple waves of air attacks followed, targeting Iranian air defences and missile sites, with the US joining approximately 30 minutes later focusing on eastern Iran and naval targets.

Significant damage was inflicted on Iran’s nuclear and military infrastructure, including destruction of missile stockpiles and command centres.

## ⚔️ Historical and Political Context

US-Iran tensions date back to the 1979 Iranian Revolution which replaced the US-backed Shah with an Islamic Republic led by Ayatollah Ruhollah Khomeini.

Iran’s opposition to Israel and pursuit of nuclear capabilities have been central to ongoing hostilities.

Attempts to curb Iran’s nuclear programme included sanctions, cyber attacks, and diplomatic efforts, notably the 2015 nuclear deal abandoned by the US in 2018.

Economic crises and mass protests in Iran intensified internal instability preceding the 2025 strikes.

## 💥 Retaliation and Regional Impact

Iran launched extensive missile attacks across the Middle East in reprisal, firing over 200 missiles at Israel and striking US and allied bases in multiple countries.

Proxy groups like the Houthis resumed attacks on shipping lanes, escalating regional conflict.

Several Gulf states including UAE, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Jordan, and Kuwait faced missile and drone strikes, causing casualties and infrastructure damage.

NATO boosted missile defences in Europe amid fears of further escalation and sleeper cell activations.

## 🌐 Legal and Global Repercussions

The US rationale for the strikes was framed as pre-emptive defence against imminent threats, though Iran’s missile range did not directly endanger the US mainland.

Critics argue the war may be illegal under international law and US constitutional norms due to lack of congressional approval.

Protests erupted globally against both Iranian regime actions and US involvement.

The conflict caused dramatic increases in oil prices and raised concerns over a global energy crisis, especially due to disruptions around the Strait of Hormuz.

## 📊 Casualties and Current Situation

Iranian casualties reportedly exceed 555 killed with hundreds injured, including military and civilians.

American forces suffered 3 fatalities and several injuries; multiple casualties occurred in Israel and allied countries.

Iran formed a provisional government signalling intent for ongoing retaliation.

The situation remains volatile with a high likelihood of further escalation in the near future.
